# Microprocessors & Microcontrollers (R23EC3202)
**Regulation**: R23 | **Semester**: 3-2 | **Credits**: 3.0 (3-0-0) | **Category**: PC

## Course Structure & Units

### Unit 1: Introduction: Basic Microprocessor architecture, Harvard and Von Neumann architectures with
examples, Microprocessor

### Unit 2: 8086 Programming: instruction set, addressing modes, assembler directives, programming with
an assembler, writing simple programs with an assembler, stack and stack structure, interrupts
and interrupt service routines 8086 system,

### Unit 3: 8086 Interfacing: Semiconductor memories interfacing (RAM, ROM), Intel 8255 programmable
peripheral interface, Interfacing switches and LEDS, Interfacing seven segment displays, Intel
8251 USART architecture and interfacing, Intel 8237a DMA controller, stepper motor, A/D and
D/A converters, Need for 8259 programmable interrupt controllers.

### Unit 4: Intel 8051 MICROCONTROLLER and Interfacing
Architecture, Hardware concepts, Input/output ports and circuits, external
memory, counters/timers, serial data input/output, interrupts. Assembly language programming:
Instructions, addressing modes, simple programs. Interfacing to 8051: A/D and D/A Convertors,
Stepper motor interface, keyboard, LCD Interfacing, Traffic light control.

### Unit 5: ersus Microcontroller Unit, History and classifications of
Microprocessor and Microcontroller.
8086 Architecture: register organization, internal architecture of 8086, pin description of 8086,
minimum mode and maximum mode of 8086 operation and timing diagrams.
