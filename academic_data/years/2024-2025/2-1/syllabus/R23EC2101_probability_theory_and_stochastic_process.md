### Unit 2: OPERATION ON ONE RANDOM VARIABLE - EXPECTATIONS
Introduction, Expected Value of a Random Variable, Function of a Random Variable, Moments about

### Unit 4: RANDOM PROCESSES - TEMPORAL CHARACTERISTICS
