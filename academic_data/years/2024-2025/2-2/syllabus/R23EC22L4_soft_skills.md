# Soft Skills (R23EC22L4)
**Regulation**: R23 | **Semester**: 2-2 | **Credits**: 2.0 (0-1-2) | **Category**: SEC

## Course Structure & Units

### Unit 1: INTRODUCTION
Introduction- Emergence of life skills, Definition & Meaning, Importance& need, reasons
for skill gap, Analysis--Soft Skills vs Hard skills, Linkage between industry and soft skills,
Challenges, Personality Developments. Soft Skills, English - Improving Techniques.

### Unit 2: Intra-Personal:
Definition-Meaning - Importance-SWOT analysis, Johari windows - Goal Setting- skills
quotient - Emotional Intelligence- Attitudinal skills - positive thinking- Problem Solving-
Time management, stress management.

### Unit 3: Inter-Personal:
Definition - Meaning - Importance-Communications skills- Team Work, managerial skills
-Negotiation skills- Leadership skills, corporate etiquettes.

### Unit 4: Verbal Skills:
Definition and Meaning -Listening skills, need - types, advantages, Importance- Tips for
Improving Listening, Speaking, need- types, advantages, Importance- Tips for Improving
Reading- Writing Skills, Report, Resume, statement of purpose, need - types, advantages,
Importance.

### Unit 5: Non Verbal Skills& Interview skills
Definition and Meaning - Importance- Facial Expressions - Eye Contact - Proxemics-
Haptics -Posture, body language in cross cultural context , body
language in interview room,  appearance and dress code - Kinetics- Para Language -
tone, pitch, pause, neutralization of accent, use of appropriate language, Interview skills,
interview methods and questions.
