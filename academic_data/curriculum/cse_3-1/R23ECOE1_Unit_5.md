# Open Elective - I (R23ECOE1) - Unit 5

Subject: Open Elective - I (R23ECOE1)
Semester: 3-1 | Credits: 3.0 | Category: OE
Unit 5: Small Signal Low Frequency Transistor Amplifier Models:
Syllabus Topics:
BJT: Two port network, Transistor hybrid model, determination of h-parameters, conversion of h-parameters,
generalized analysis of transistor amplifier model using h-parameters, Analysis of CB, CE and CC amplifiers
using exact and approximate analysis, Comparison of transistor amplifiers.
FET: Generalized analysis of small signal model, Analysis of CG, CS and CD amplifiers, comparison of FET
amplifiers.
Course Learning Outcome: CO5: Understand and apply concepts of Small Signal Low Frequency Transistor Amplifier Models:
Institution: Narasaraopeta Engineering College (Autonomous), Regulation: R23
