Choosing a form of business organization specific to your venture, identifying sources of funds: Debt & Equity, Map the Start-up Lifecycle to Funding Options. Core Teaching Tool: Founder Case Studies – Sama and SecurelyShare; Class activity and discussions; Venture Activities. Page 4 of 6
