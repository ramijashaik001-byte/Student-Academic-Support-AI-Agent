# Microprocessors & Microcontrollers (R23EC3202) - Unit 4

Subject: Microprocessors & Microcontrollers (R23EC3202)
Semester: 3-2 | Credits: 3.0 | Category: PC
Unit 4: Intel 8051 MICROCONTROLLER and Interfacing
Syllabus Topics:
Architecture, Hardware concepts, Input/output ports and circuits, external
memory, counters/timers, serial data input/output, interrupts. Assembly language programming:
Instructions, addressing modes, simple programs. Interfacing to 8051: A/D and D/A Convertors,
Stepper motor interface, keyboard, LCD Interfacing, Traffic light control.
Course Learning Outcome: CO4: Understand and apply concepts of Intel 8051 MICROCONTROLLER and Interfacing
Institution: Narasaraopeta Engineering College (Autonomous), Regulation: R23
